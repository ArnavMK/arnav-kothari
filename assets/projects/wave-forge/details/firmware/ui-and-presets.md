## The interface

A screen gives the amp a modern digital look: tones, knobs and parameters, all editable in real time.

## Presets

- A preset stores **every amp and cab parameter**, plus which impulse response is loaded
- Presets can be **saved and loaded** at any time

## MIDI footswitch mapping

The UI shows a **2D mockup of the pedal**. Tap a switch, pick a preset, done. When the pedal sends MIDI with a matching value, the amp recalls that preset.
