# Gen — Power Supply

> **Placeholder.** Add the schematic, layout notes and bring-up results.

**Gen Rev 2** is the power supply board, and the first PCB I ever assembled with small components.

![Gen Rev 2 assembled](assets/projects/wave-forge/Timeline/Gen_pcb_1.jpeg)

## Assembly

The boards and parts came from **JLCPCB** and **DigiKey**. I reflowed Gen on a **hotplate with solder paste**.

## What went well

- A really good first pass for a first hotplate reflow

## What to watch

- The board wasn't perfectly flush with the hotplate, so solder melted at different rates
- One resistor flipped from its original position, and a few passives were misaligned
- A potential short to check before power-on

## Bring-up checklist

- [ ] Visual inspection under magnification
- [ ] Continuity checks for shorts
- [ ] First power-up with a current limit
- [ ] Measure each rail
