## What is Wave Forge?

Wave Forge is a custom **solid-state guitar amplifier** built from scratch — hardware *and* firmware. It's a multi-board, feature-dense amp split into an **amp head** and a **cabinet**, aiming to be a Quad Cortex on the inside and a traditional amp on the outside.

## Why build it?

- I've played electric guitar for over 10 years, and I get most of my tones through Neural DSP plugins on a laptop
- That means a laptop, an audio interface, the guitar and a speaker — a lot of hassle at a live gig
- It's also the first real **end-to-end project** to teach me audio hardware and embedded firmware properly
